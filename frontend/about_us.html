About Bangla Karneval e.V.

Bringing the Bengali community together in Wesseling, Germany

Our Story

Bangla Karneval e.V. celebrates Bengali culture with traditional performances, food, and community in the heart of Germany.

We bring Bengali families across North Rhine-Westphalia together to celebrate our heritage, language, and traditions.
