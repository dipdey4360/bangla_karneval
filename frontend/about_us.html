About Bangla Karneval e.V.

A shared home for Bengali culture and community in Cologne, Germany.

Our Story

Our journey began in a small pub in Cologne—a place where people came together to share their music. Sitting there, we wondered: with such a rich musical heritage and vibrant culture, why weren’t we sharing the sounds of Bengal too?

So we organised an evening of Bengali music in that same pub, expecting a small gathering. What followed exceeded our hopes. More people came than we had imagined, filling the room with warmth, excitement and a love for the music that connected us all.

Your response gave us the courage to dream bigger. That first evening became the beginning of something more: a community celebrating its roots while building a home together, far from home.

We wanted that feeling to last beyond one event each year. From Eid and Puja to Noboborsho and informal gatherings, we imagined bringing people together to share traditions, make memories and welcome new friendships.

To bring these celebrations under one roof, we formed Bangla Karneval e.V.—with you, and for you. At its heart is the same hope that brought us to that small pub: to celebrate our culture together and pass that sense of belonging to the next generation.
