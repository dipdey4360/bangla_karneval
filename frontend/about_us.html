About Bangla Karneval

Bringing the Bengali community together in Wesseling, Germany

Our Story

Bangla Karneval celebrates Bengali culture with traditional performances, food, and community in the heart of Germany.

Every year we bring together Bengali families from across North Rhine-Westphalia to celebrate our shared heritage, language, and traditions.
